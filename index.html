The one-line answer

Extending P41 bought +5.9pp more tasks actioned and cut P41 deaths by 15.1pp. But CSP responsiveness inside the first two working hours is identical across the two weeks (33.1% vs 32.8%). The gain is entirely incremental accrual between hour 2 and hour 6 — work that used to be guillotined before the CSP got to it.

The change is real in the config

Verified directly rather than assumed: the configured minimum P41_DEADLINE_AT − CREATED_AT steps from exactly 2.0h to exactly 6.0h on 22 July. But the effective window is far longer than 6h for most tasks, because the deadline rolls to the next working day when it would otherwise expire after hours.

Do not read POST's higher percentiles as "CSPs got slower"

PRE's distribution is right-truncated by its own deadline. Under a 2h window you cannot observe a five-hour slot proposal — the task was already killed. So PRE's flattering P90 of 1.36 working hours is largely an artifact of the guillotine, not evidence of faster CSPs. The censoring-consistent comparison is the cumulative curve above and the action rate, not the conditional percentiles.

Two caveats that constrain the read

1 · 43% of post-change assignments are not a CSP acting

647 of 1,500 assignments complete within 60 seconds of candidate creation. Tracing them: the DAS allocation, candidate creation, SLOT_CONFIRMED_BY_CUSTOMER and AWAITING_TECHNICIAN_ASSIGNMENT all land within ~2 seconds of each other, then TECHNICIAN_ASSIGNED fires 6–52 seconds later.

The actors are real, varying CSP account IDs and the downstream outcomes are genuine (CONNECTION_ACTIVE, CANCELLED_BY_CUSTOMER, INSTALLATION_REPORTED_FAILED) — so this is not test data. But no human assigns a technician in 30 seconds. The sequence is being replayed into the new stack rather than performed on it. The mechanism could not be determined from these tables alone; it needs an engineering answer. A comparable cluster exists in PRE (742 of 4,985), so the cohort comparison stays fair — but any percentile computed over the unfiltered set is meaningless.

2 · P41 is confounded with the customer-scheduling launch

A new customer-scheduling flow went live ~21 July, one day before the P41 change — new state AWAITING_CUSTOMER_SCHEDULE, new SLOT_CONFIRMATION_SOURCE = 'CUSTOMER_PROPOSED'. It is now 82% of the cohort (3,165 of 3,850) and 88% of assignments. The customer picks the slot up front, so candidates arrive with the slot already confirmed and skip CSP slot proposal entirely.

This is also why the two cohorts need different action definitions: in PRE the CSP's first required step was proposing a slot; in POST it is assigning a technician. Neither week can be cleanly attributed to P41 alone.

Methodology

Definitions and cohort construction
  • Cohort anchor — task/candidate creation date (CREATED_AT, IST), tracked forward to whenever the action occurred. Not event-date. One row per EXECUTION_CANDIDATE_ID.
  • PRE = created 14–20 Jul 2026 (4,985 candidates). POST = created 26 Jul–1 Aug 2026 (3,850).
  • Clock start ("notif") — CREATED_AT, the moment the task is pushed to the CSP. This is the exact anchor P41 is measured from.
  • Clock end, PRE — first OCCURRED_AT in INSTALL_STATE_TRANSITION_LOG with ACTOR_TYPE='CSP' and TO_STATE IN ('SLOT_SELECTED','AWAITING_CUSTOMER_SLOT_CONFIRMATION'). Both are recorded at the same instant; taking the earlier of the two avoids a tie-break bias.
  • Clock end, POST — first TO_STATE='TECHNICIAN_ASSIGNED', excluding TECHNICIAN_ASSIGNED → TECHNICIAN_ASSIGNED reassignments.
  • Working hours — 9 AM – 9 PM IST, 12h/day, counted on all seven days (field installs run weekends). Computed as a monotonic working-time coordinate: day_index × 43200 + clamp(seconds since 09:00, 0, 43200), then differenced. Validated against worked examples — e.g. created 30 Jul 16:06 → assigned 31 Jul 12:18 = 20.2 calendar hours = 4.9h + 3.3h = 8.2 working hours.
  • Declines excluded from "actioned" — a decline is a CSP response but not progress. Reported separately.
  • Right-censoring — negligible for POST: only 23 of 1,500 assignments took >24h, and the 1 Aug cohort had ≥48h observed as of 3 Aug.

Open questions for engineering
  • What produces the sub-60-second assignment cluster? 647 post-change and 742 pre-change candidates have their entire chain replayed at ingest. Until this is explained, every latency metric on this funnel needs it filtered out.
  • Should P41 be measured on a working-hours clock explicitly? The deadline already rolls over for after-hours expiry, which is why the effective median window is ~16 calendar hours rather than 6. Stating the parameter in working hours would make the config match its behaviour.
  • P41 is still the top failure mode at 6h (1,441 candidates, 61% of non-actioned). The curve flattens hard after hour 6, so a further extension looks low-yield — the remaining loss is unlikely to be a timing problem.
